Gaṇita Bhāratī

The peer-reviewed bulletin of the Indian Society for History of Mathematics, devoted to publication of significant original articles in the history of mathematics and related areas.

About the Journal

Gaṇita Bhāratī is devoted to the publication of significant original articles in the history of mathematics and related areas. Although English is the official language of the journal, an article of exceptional merit written in French, German, Sanskrit or Hindi will also be considered as a special case.

Effective Vol. 28 (2006), Gaṇita Bhāratī is published bi-annually in the months of June and December. Apart from continuing its emphasis on articles on the history of ancient Indian mathematics, the Bulletin welcomes contributions on any aspect of the world history of mathematics, including:

  • General Histories, Source Books and Biographies of Mathematicians
  • Mathematics and Indigenous Cultures of the World
  • The Origin of Mathematics
  • 19th and 20th Century Mathematics
  • Renaissance and the History of Mathematics in Education Curricula

The journal also carries Review and Survey articles, Classroom Notes, Dissertation Abstracts, Book Reviews, Letters to the Editor, Opinions, News Café, International Events and Obituaries.

The corresponding author of an article published in GB will be entitled to receive that issue free, provided they bear a nominal charge of ₹ 150.00 (within India) or US$ 15.00 (outside India) to cover handling and shipping. Authors also receive a PDF of their published work free of cost by e-mail.

Call for Submissions

Original contributions to the history of mathematics are welcomed year-round.

Gaṇita Bhāratī invites original research articles, review and survey papers, classroom notes, dissertation abstracts and book reviews on the history of mathematics, with particular emphasis on the mathematical traditions of India, while welcoming contributions on any aspect of the world history of mathematics.

All submissions are peer-reviewed. The bulletin is published bi-annually, in June and December. English is the official language of the journal; an article of exceptional merit written in French, German, Sanskrit or Hindi will also be considered as a special case.

How to Submit

  • Prepare your manuscript using the official template (see below).
  • Submit the article as a PDF together with an editable source file.
  • Include an abstract in English, along with complete author affiliation and contact details.

Manuscripts and editorial correspondence may be addressed to the Editor (see the Editorial Board below). Submission email: to be confirmed.
