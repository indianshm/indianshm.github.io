Types of Membership

Founder

Persons whose names appear in the Memorandum of Association as desirous of forming the Society are regarded as Founder Members. They pay membership dues as prescribed for ordinary or life members.

Ordinary

Any person interested in the objects of the Society and agreeing to abide by its constitution and byelaws is eligible for election as an ordinary member. (Currently not available for persons from SAARC countries — life membership only.)

Life

Any person eligible for election as an ordinary member is also eligible to apply for life membership, which confers permanent standing with all associated rights and privileges.

Honorary

Honorary membership is by invitation only. The Council may nominate distinguished persons of eminence for a period of two years, provided the total number of honorary members does not at any time exceed five.

Rights and Obligations

All members shall be entitled to:

  • Receive communications about the activities of the Society
  • Participate in annual conferences without paying sessional fees
  • Have free access to the online issues of Gaṇita Bhāratī
  • Stand for election as an office-bearer or member of the Council

Any member whose subscription is in arrears for two consecutive years shall cease to enjoy the rights and privileges of membership.
