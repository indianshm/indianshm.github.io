Executive Council

Governing Body

The affairs of the Society are managed by an elected Executive Council comprising the office bearers and members elected at the Annual Conference.

Office Bearers

As per decision of the Executive Council meeting, December 2019.

KR
President
Prof. K. Ramasubramanian
Department of Humanities & Social Sciences
Indian Institute of Technology, Mumbai
Prof. K. Ramasubramanian is a distinguished scholar of Indian astronomy and mathematics. His research encompasses the mathematical and astronomical traditions of ancient and medieval India, with particular focus on the Kerala school of mathematics.
AJ
Vice-President
Prof. Anupam Jain
Government Holkar Science College
Indore
Prof. Anupam Jain specialises in Jain mathematics and the history of Indian mathematical traditions. He has contributed extensively to research on ancient Indian numerical systems and mathematical texts.
DS
General Secretary
Prof. Dinesh Singh
Department of Mathematics
University of Delhi, Delhi
Prof. Dinesh Singh is a mathematician and former Vice Chancellor of the University of Delhi. Son of ISHM founder Prof. U. N. Singh, he has been a driving force in the Society's leadership and the promotion of mathematics education across India.

Prof. M. C. Joshi has served the Society in various administrative capacities and has contributed to the organisation of national conferences and symposia on the history of mathematics.
